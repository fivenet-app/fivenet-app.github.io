## Overview

The iOS app "FiveNet Mobile" is an open community effort around a mobile app for FiveNet users.
It is developed separately from the main FiveNet application and is tracked publicly on GitHub.

The main developer is [PhilTec-Philip](https://github.com/PhilTec-Philip).

## Repository

Follow the upstream work here: [github.com/fivenet-app/app-ios](https://github.com/fivenet-app/app-ios)
